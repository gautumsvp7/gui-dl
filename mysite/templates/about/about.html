About CrowdVision

Crowd management tools haven't changed much. A clicker at the door, a basic entry sensor — you get a number, maybe, but nothing else. No spatial awareness, no warning when density is climbing toward dangerous.

We're a team of students from UTS Sydney, and we built CrowdVision as a deep learning project. But the problem we were solving is real. Crowd crushes and stampedes aren't freak accidents — they're the result of density building up faster than anyone noticed, in spaces where no one had the right information.

Most of those incidents are preventable with better information.

CrowdVision gives venues that information. Connect your existing CCTV infrastructure and within seconds you get a live count and a heatmap showing exactly where people are concentrated — not just how many, but where. And when density crosses a dangerous threshold, CrowdVision doesn't wait for someone to notice. It sends an automatic alert instantly, so your team can act before it becomes a crisis — whether that means opening another exit, redirecting foot traffic, or calling for extra staff.

You don't need to replace anything. Most venues already have cameras everywhere — CrowdVision just makes them smarter.
