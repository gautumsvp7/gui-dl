About CrowdVision

We started CrowdVision because the tools venues use to manage crowds haven't really changed. A person at the door with a clicker, or a basic entry sensor that tells you a number but nothing else. No map, no hotspots, no warning before things get dangerous.

We're a team of students from UTS Sydney who built this as a deep learning project, but the problem we set out to solve is very real. Crowd crushes and stampedes aren't freak accidents. They are the result of density building up faster than anyone noticed.

Most of those incidents are preventable with better information.

CrowdVision gives venues that information. Connect your existing CCTV infrastructure and within seconds you get a live count and a heatmap showing exactly where people are concentrated. Not just how many, but where. That's the part that matters when you need to decide whether to open another exit, redirect foot traffic, or call for extra staff.

The key is that you don't need to rip out or replace anything. Most venues already have cameras everywhere — CrowdVision just makes them smarter.
